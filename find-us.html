Where To Find Us

Purple Door Ice Cream: Walker's Point Scoop Shop & Production Kitchen

205 S 2nd St. Milwaukee, WI 53204

414-988-2521

Stop by Purple Door's retail scoop shop in Historic Walker's Point for a scoop of hand-crafted Purple Door ice cream in a cone or cup, or as a shake or sundae. Our Walker's Point Shop has been scooping ice cream since 2014, offering 20 ice cream options, with flavors rotating daily. While here, glance through the 2nd Street window into the Purple Door production kitchen to see ice cream being made.

Purple Door Ice Cream: Mequon Public Market

6300 W Mequon Rd Mequon, WI 53092

262-643-4073

Stop by Purple Door's newest location on the North Shore area of Milwaukee. The Mequon Public Market @ Spur 16 opened in June, 2019. The Public Market offers diverse dining choices for every member of the family. Enjoy a scoop of ice cream cone on the beautiful outdoor patio or the spacious mezzanine that overlooks the entire indoor market.
